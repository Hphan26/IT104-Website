IT 104 Course Syllabus

Introduction to Computing
Fall 2026 — George Mason University

Objective

IT 104 introduces foundational computing concepts, including hardware, software, networks, databases, web development, cybersecurity, and emerging technologies such as generative AI. Students learn to evaluate digital information and to use computing tools effectively and responsibly.

Textbooks

  • Computers: Understanding Technology, Kendall/Hunt, e-book (ISBN 9798385108183).
  • Computer Concepts and Applications, Kendall/Hunt, e-book (ISBN 9798385108176).
